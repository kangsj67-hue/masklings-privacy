개발자는 게임 진행 데이터를 자체 서버로 수집하거나 개인정보를 판매하지 않습니다.

앱은 계정, 자체 분석, 위치, 연락처, 사진, 카메라 또는 마이크 기능을 사용하지 않습니다.

앱을 삭제하면 운영체제 정책에 따라 기기에 저장된 게임 진행 데이터도 삭제됩니다. 운영체제 백업을 사용하는 경우 로컬 앱 데이터가 기기 백업에 포함될 수 있습니다.

1. Scope

This policy applies to MASKLINGS: RUSH, provided by KCREW.

2. Personal data collection

The developer does not collect game progress on its own servers or sell personal data.

The app uses Pangle interstitial advertising. Pangle may process IP addresses, device and advertising identifiers, and app and advertising usage information to deliver ads, prevent fraud, and measure performance. In the EEA, United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the app presents a personalized-ad consent choice. Declining does not prevent use of the game or non-personalized ads.

The app does not use accounts, first-party analytics, location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone features.

3. Local game data

Stage unlocks, best times, collection records, tutorial completion, audio settings, and advertising consent choices are stored on the user's device.

Deleting the app also removes locally stored game progress according to the operating system's behavior. Local app data may be included in a device backup when operating-system backup is enabled.
