기본 확인

앱을 완전히 종료한 뒤 다시 실행하고 최신 버전인지 확인해 주세요. 게임 진행과 설정은 기기 내부에 저장되므로 앱을 삭제하거나 앱 데이터를 지우면 기록이 제거될 수 있습니다.

Need help?

For issues with launching the game, saved progress, controls, or puzzles, contact us at admin@kcrew.co.kr.

Please include your device model, operating-system version, room number, and steps to reproduce the issue. Do not send sensitive information such as account credentials or passwords.

Quick checks

Fully close and reopen the App, and make sure you are using the latest version. Progress and settings are stored locally, so uninstalling the App or clearing its data may remove your records.
